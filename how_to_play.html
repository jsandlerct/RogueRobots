Rogue Robots

FIELD GUIDE — WRITTEN FOR YOU BEFORE WE GO IN

Hey. I'm the one who got you into this building, remember? So you're going to trust me on this.

The facility's AI has spread itself across a series of servers deep inside the complex. Each one is protected by waves of combat robots that it controls. Your job — our job — is to fight through them, floor by floor, and destroy each server before it destroys ours.

I've mapped out how the system works. Read this before we go in. It's not complicated once you understand the loop.

— your partner

What's Going On

Each floor is a tug-of-war on a grid. The map is split in two — the AI controls the top half, you control the bottom. Both sides have a server in the corner. Both sides spawn robots that fight each other on the way to the enemy server.

Your goal: destroy the AI's server before it destroys yours. Your robots fight automatically once deployed — your job is deciding which robots to bring and when to put them on the field.

In other words
Think of it like chess, but the pieces run off on their own the moment you put them down. Which is either terrifying or liberating, depending on your mood.

The Map

The grid is 12 columns × 16 rows. The yellow line across the middle is the territorial divide.

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐ │ ★ AI SERVER (top-left) AI TERRITORY │ │ │ │ Rows 1–8: Enemy robots spawn and roam │ │ here, pathing toward your server. │ │ │ │━━━━━━━━━━━ TERRITORIAL DIVIDE ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━│ │ │ │ Rows 9–16: Your territory. │ │ Your robots spawn here. │ │ │ │ Your robots spawn from YOUR SERVER │ │ (bottom-right corner) ★ YOUR SERVER │ └─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Drop-type units — towers, traps, walls — are placed anywhere on your side of the divide. Base-type units spawn automatically from your server corner and march toward the AI.

Important
You cannot place Drop units on the AI's side of the map. The divide is the hard limit. Drop units go in rows 9–16 only.

Resources

You have three currencies. Every unit costs some combination of them to deploy.

🔩
⬡ Metal
Used by almost everything. Dropped by destroyed enemy robots.
◈ Silicon
For smart, technical, or ranged units. Found as map tokens.
⚡ Batteries
Powers energy weapons and advanced units. Found as map tokens.

Metal is the critical resource. Aside from the Datamine tower, the only way to earn Metal mid-round is by destroying enemy robots. Silicon and Batteries appear as glowing tokens on the map — your units collect them by walking over them. The Scavenger bot is specifically built to rush out and grab tokens. The Datamine tower generates all three resources passively over time.

You also start each floor with a bonus resource pool that scales with your level, so later floors give you more to work with from the start.

The Prep Screen

Before each floor, you choose your loadout — up to 8 units. This is the most important decision you make each round.

  1. You see a grid of all units you've unlocked so far. More units unlock as you level up.
  2. Drag a unit card into one of the loadout slots at the bottom to add it. Click a filled slot to remove it.
  3. The order of your slots matters — your most-used units should be easiest to reach.
  4. Once you're happy, hit CONFIRM ▶ to start the round.
Note
The screen shows the AI's designation, their favorite robot, and a quote. The favorite robot is the one they'll deploy most aggressively — build your loadout around countering it.
My tip
I always check the enemy's favorite unit first. If it's a Cannonbot, I bring fast cheap units to swarm it before it can fire. If it's a Medibot, I bring burst damage to punch through the healing. Don't just bring the same loadout every floor.

Deploying Units

Base Units BASE

These deploy from your server corner and pathfind automatically toward the AI's server. Click their slot in the loadout bar at the bottom to deploy one. There's a 1-second cooldown between any deployments.

Drop Units DROP

These are placed by you on your half of the map. Click the slot, then click a tile on the board to place it. This includes towers, traps, walls, and support units.

Watch out
Placing a Drop unit on a walkable path tile will block movement for all units. Sometimes this is intentional (Wallbot as a damage sponge), but usually it isn't. Think before you place.

Bugs — your free auto-spawner

Bugs are the baseline unit: 3 HP, 1 damage, medium speed. They spawn automatically from your server every 4 seconds at no cost. You don't draft them — they just show up. The AI spawns them constantly too, and at an escalating rate as the round goes on.

Time pressure
At 5 minutes the AI's Bug spawn rate doubles. At 10 minutes it quadruples. Don't let rounds drag — the longer it goes, the harder their push becomes.

Powerups

During a round, powerup tokens occasionally drop on the map. Walk a unit over one to collect it into one of your 3 powerup slots (left panel). Click a slot to activate it.

EMP
Stuns all enemy robots for 5 seconds.
Airstrike
Destroys every enemy unit currently on your side of the map.
Overcharge
Your robots deal double damage for 10 seconds.
Supply Drop
Instantly gain 10 Metal, 5 Silicon, 5 Batteries.
+
Surge
Immediately spawns 5 of your auto-spawn robot.
Fortify
Your server takes zero damage for 20 seconds.
My tip
I save EMP and Airstrike for emergencies — specifically when a wave of enemy units is about to reach the server. Supply Drop is best used early so you can afford to deploy more units right away. Fortify is most valuable when the server is already taking damage and you need time to push back.

Progression

You earn XP by destroying enemy robots and for each floor you complete. Level up to unlock more unit types and more loadout slots (up to 8 total). There are 15 floors in a full run, and a level cap of 25.

Each floor has a named AI opponent with their own favorite robot and a quote. Harder floors give you more starting resources to compensate.

Passive Skills
At certain levels (10, 15, 20, 23, 25) you unlock a passive skill. The Scavenging line gives enemy units a chance to drop bonus resources when you destroy them — more kills means more income. Each tier is stronger than the last: early tiers give a single random resource per kill, and by level 20 you're pulling two random resources per kill. The level 25 skill is something special — I'm not going to spoil it.

A Few More Tips

From me
Don't just spam Punchbots. Mix in some ranged units or you'll never punch through an armored line.
From me
A Datamine placed early pays for itself in under a minute. Just make sure it's not sitting on the front line — it can't defend itself at all.
From me
Floatbot bypasses everything on the ground. Send one straight over a clogged enemy front line. Just remember it can only be hit by units with range greater than 1, so the AI will adapt.
Most important
You'll lose floors. That's fine. XP and levels carry over between runs. Keep going.
